## Product boundary and v0.9 direction

First Pass extracts deterministic, local evidence. It works best when a ticket
states the affected population, failed business operation, required-by event,
deadline driver, available path, harm timing, and containment explicitly. It
conservatively leaves relationships unknown when they are implied across
clauses or depend on equivalence judgement.

**v0.9.0 Safe Next Action (documented only):** a next action may use explicit
current primary evidence or analyst-confirmed evidence. It must choose
**Clarify** when business consequence, deadline relation, workaround
equivalence, multi-clause composition, or evidence authority is uncertain. It
must never alter P1–P4.
