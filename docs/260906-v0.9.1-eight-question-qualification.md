## Scope

This release clarifies the analyst-facing model as exactly eight Decision Questions
(I1–I4/U5–U8). It adds contract coverage for the stable IDs and evidence ownership,
and exposes already-supported explicit recoverability beneath I3. No extraction,
scoring, policy, matrix, P1–P4, Safe Next Action, dependency, backend, or deployment
semantics were intentionally changed.
