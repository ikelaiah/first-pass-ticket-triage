# v0.11.0 — Capability-Boundary Burn-Down, Generalisation, and Holdout Unseal

**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Baseline:** v0.10.0 `main` at `f263c34`
**Branch:** `release/v0.11.0-capability-burndown`
**Scope:** local deterministic triage accuracy, safety gating, generalisation,
and the one-time final-holdout unseal

## Executive summary

v0.11.0 closes every Category B capability boundary (9 of 9), resolves three
Category C cases and one Category D case, documents the seven remaining C cases
as policy or label boundaries, adds a 33-test generalisation and adversarial
suite, and unseals the frozen 24-case final holdout once. The holdout failed its
first look on safety, fourteen engine defects were fixed, and the post-fix
regression reads 23/24 with perfect P1 precision and recall. The matrix,
evidence → Impact + Urgency pipeline, and the eight Decision Questions are
unchanged.

2026-09-20. The first look is the verdict:

The two safety-critical false negatives were active disclosure of one
household's balance to another household and "can still open" welfare records
after a clearance ended. Full adjudication and the replacement-holdout contract
are in [the holdout qualification](260920-final-holdout-qualification.md).

## Release contents

- **Adjudication:** `docs/260919-safety-boundary-adjudication.md` resolves four
  disputed labels against the written policy; the stale v0.8.0 calibration
  report carries an erratum.
- **Safety gate:** `node tests/evaluate.mjs` fails on unsafe or severe
  under-prioritisation, P1 false negatives, and assessed-ticket abstentions.
- **Evidence and policy:** payroll-file failure, skipped/omitted records,
  accessibility barriers, recoverable loss (`loss.recoverable` as written),
  recurrence and propagation bounds, stale displays, blocked lodgement,
  costly workarounds, active exposure wording, pending privacy drafts,
  resolved-since context, negated service failures, paused-job continuity, and
  an unrecoverable-loss urgency floor.
- **Generalisation:** `tests/generalisation-tests.js` re-tests every fix with
  unseen wording and asserts urgency monotonicity, comparator and history scope,
  clause-local negation, and adversarial inputs.
- **Holdout:** consumed once, with first-look metadata, checksum, and a
  re-runnable regression harness.
