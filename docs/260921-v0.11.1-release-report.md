# v0.11.1 — Replacement Holdout Measurement

**Date:** 2026-09-21
**Baseline:** v0.11.0 `main` at `d3ec473`
**Scope:** one-time unseal of the independently labelled replacement holdout,
the safety and facet fixes it exposed, and release qualification

## Executive summary

v0.11.1 is a safety release driven by the v0.11.1 replacement holdout. The
holdout failed its first look with three unsafe under-prioritisations and four
severe under-prioritisations; all twenty-two engine defects it exposed were
fixed, and the post-fix regression reads 24/24 exact priority with perfect P1
precision and recall. The evidence → Impact + Urgency pipeline, the 3×3 matrix,
and the eight Decision Questions are unchanged. No dependency, backend, network
call, LLM, telemetry, or persistence was added.

## Replacement holdout

The fixture was independently authored and labelled, labelled before
evaluation, and unsealed once. **The first look is the verdict.**

The safety-critical findings were a chemical hazard outside the support
boundary, a suspended teacher who could still message pupils, permanently
erased records with no surviving copy, destroyed board minutes with no
restoration path, "neither … nor … available" read as a workaround, and a
regulator portal closing in ninety minutes. Full adjudication is in
[the replacement holdout qualification](260920-replacement-holdout-qualification.md).

## Fixes in this release

- **Safety:** chemical hazards are in scope; "can still send" is
  access-not-revoked; erased or destroyed loss with no recovery path is
  unrecoverable; "neither … nor … available" is no workaround; "in ninety
  minutes" and "in two hours" are same-day deadlines.
- **Evidence:** regulator statutory driver, export and feed failure impairment,
  individual receptionist scope, "no substitute", partial
  works-for-remaining, manual daily cost, paused-feed continuity, days-from-now
  bucketing, bare-participle deletion harm, explicit preference wording,
  "no account change" no longer missing data, pending "awaiting review", and
  learning-support privacy context.
- **Policy input:** a soft-continuation signal ("can continue meanwhile") keeps
  a recoverable loss from being priced as an imminent restoration need.
- **Guards:** eight unseen-paraphrase generalisation tests cover the safety
  fixes; a work-in-progress holdout validator was added.
